How it works

Shared infrastructure for the network.

SuperBenefit is made up of small, self-directed teams working on shared problems. Each project stays in charge of its funds and decisions. But no one has to build everything from scratch.

Bring your own organization, anchor in our DAO, or pass through. Leave anytime.

Supports

Peer support between cells. No prices.

People to work with

Find the people your project needs to keep moving.

Facilitators, contributors and stewards across the network can step in for coordination, decision support and ongoing work.

Shared infrastructure

Use the infrastructure other projects already depend on.

The DAO, shared tooling, documentation templates and patterns you can adapt instead of inventing. Take what you need, contribute what you build, and keep what you make.

Funding support

Apply for grants with input from people who have done it before.

Connect with fiscal hosts in the network who can move funds where they need to go. Share reporting approaches and learn from other projects, so no one starts from scratch.

Admin and paperwork

Use our DAO and existing entity arrangements when you need them.

We help with the admin, filings and coordination that keep a project running, without requiring you to stand up your own back office.
