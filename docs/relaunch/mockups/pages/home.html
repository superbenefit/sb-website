About SuperBenefit

We're a network of small, autonomous teams using emerging technologies for local impact.

SuperBenefit is made up of small, self-directed teams, called cells, working on shared problems. Each one stays in charge of its own funds and decisions. They share people, tools and practical support, so no one has to build everything from scratch.

Current work includes All In For Sport, DAO Primitives and the Institute for Community Sustainability.
